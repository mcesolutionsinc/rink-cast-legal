How it works

Set your teams up once. On game day, it takes a few taps to tell everyone what just happened.

  1. 1Build your roster once

    Add each team's players with their jersey numbers. Rink Cast keeps them for every game after.

  2. 2Call it live

    Tap what happened, then the team and player. The announcement is written for you — check the preview and tap Send.

  3. 3Everyone knows instantly

    Pick your team's chat and it's posted. Families who couldn't make it follow along play by play.

Everything a game needs

Built for the person with a phone in one hand and a coffee in the other.

Every kind of call

Goals with assists, penalties, shots, saves, icing, offside, fights and timeouts — plus period starts, one-minute warnings and back to full strength.

Any chat app

Announcements go through your phone's share menu, so they work with WhatsApp, Messages, or whichever app your team already uses.

Fix a mistake

Tapped the wrong player? Remove the entry from the game log and the score updates to match.

Picks up where you left off

A phone call or a flat battery won't cost you the game. Reopen the app and resume right where you were.

Share rosters

Export your teams to a file and import them on another phone, so a co-coach or the next parent can take over.

Hold it either way

Works in portrait or landscape, however you're standing at the glass.

A full recap when the horn goes

When the game's over, Rink Cast pulls everything together into one summary and sends it in a single tap.

  • Final score and who won
  • Every goal, with scorers and assists
  • Every penalty, by period

Your rosters stay on your phone

There's no account to create and nothing to sign in to. Team names and players are stored on your device, and Rink Cast doesn't run ads or analytics. Read the privacy policy.
