Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy covers the apps and services published by MC eSolutions Inc, including Rink Cast. It explains what data we collect, why, and how it's handled.

The short version

Rink Cast stores your team rosters on your own device. We don't run our own servers, we don't have user accounts, and we don't collect analytics or advertising data. The only outside parties involved are the app stores and our subscription-billing provider, who handle your purchase — not us.

Data stored on your device

Team names, jersey numbers, and player names you enter are stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This data is never transmitted to MC eSolutions Inc or to any third party automatically. It stays on your device unless you take a deliberate action to move it — for example:

  • Exporting a team file to share or save it yourself (you choose the destination — a messaging app, email, cloud storage, or a folder on your device).
  • Sending a game announcement through your device's share sheet (for example, to WhatsApp) — you choose the recipient each time; we never send anything on your behalf.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data through your device's app settings, permanently deletes everything stored locally. Because we don't keep a copy anywhere else, there's nothing further for us to delete on our end.

Subscription and payment data

Rink Cast offers a free trial followed by a paid subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, and managed on our behalf by RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. We never see or store your payment card details.

To manage your subscription and confirm your entitlement, RevenueCat and the app stores process information such as a device- or account-linked purchase identifier and your subscription status (active, trialing, expired, etc.). This is standard for any app-store subscription and is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs in Rink Cast.
  • We don't require an account, login, or personal profile to use the app.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Team rosters may include a minor's name and jersey number entered by a coach or parent using the app — this data stays on that person's own device under the same terms described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Since we don't collect contact information from users, we're not able to notify you directly — please check back if you have concerns.
